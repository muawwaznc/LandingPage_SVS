Smart Ventilation System

An IoT-based solution to monitor and improve indoor air quality. Our system automatically adjusts ventilation components like exhaust fans and windows based on real-time readings of CO₂, temperature, humidity, and particulate matter.

About the Project

The Smart Ventilation System is designed to maintain healthy and comfortable indoor air quality by monitoring key environmental parameters and responding automatically. It’s ideal for homes, offices, and healthcare spaces where air quality directly affects well-being.

IoT-Enabled Monitoring

Real-time measurement of CO₂, temperature, humidity, and particulate matter using precise IoT sensors.

Automatic Control

Automatically adjusts ventilation components such as exhaust fans or windows to maintain ideal air quality.

User Customization

Allows users to set preferred air quality thresholds and view real-time stats on a connected mobile app.
